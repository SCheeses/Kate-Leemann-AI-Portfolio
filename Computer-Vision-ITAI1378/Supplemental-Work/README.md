# Supplemental Computer Vision Work

I include these assignments to provide context for my completed notebook projects and demonstrate my research, technical writing, visual communication, and development-tool reflection.

## [Computer Vision in Medical Image Analysis](Medical_Image_Analysis.pdf)

I researched medical-image pipelines, common computer-vision techniques, workflow benefits, evaluation gaps, and the importance of human oversight. I connected published evidence to my experience in genetics research and emphasized that regulatory clearance does not replace real-world clinical validation.

## [A Royal Feast of Image Formats](Royal_Feast_of_Image_Formats.pdf)

I used a medieval banquet metaphor to explain RAW, JPEG, BMP, PNG, TIFF, HEIF, and AVIF. Through the visual design, I compared compression, image quality, compatibility, transparency, and appropriate use cases.

## [GitHub and Jupyter Workflow Reflection](GitHub_Jupyter_Workflow_Reflection.pdf)

I reflected on creating my first Jupyter repository, configuring Visual Studio Code, running notebook cells, committing my work, and learning how `.gitignore` prevents unintended operating-system files from being tracked.
